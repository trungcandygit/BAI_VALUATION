Comments:

Editor:

1. Emphasis on originality and clearer contribution: Please revise the paper in a way that makes its contribution clearer for the reader. You need to motivate your paper in relation to the literature and rewrite wherever possible using simple economic intuitions, including the introduction, to clearly state the purpose of the paper and additional contributions. Please also have a look at past issues of the Southeast Asian Journal of Economics and cite papers that are relevant to your topic.

2. Abstract: Please write the abstract as clearly as possible, along with the key economic arguments for the ideas you are exploring (you should try to keep it at about 150 words or less). Sometimes when the abstract is hard to comprehend or remains technical, an average reader avoids reading the paper. Thus, it would be in your own interest to make the abstract clear, including the last line as a general forward-looking conclusion on the topic.

3. Title page and references: In your revised version, the title page or cover page of the main document should include an acknowledgements footnote, acknowledging comments received from the reviewer(s) and editor(s) in this footnote. Please make sure that your references are up-to-date and have complete citation details. It would be good to check your references and, where relevant, remove very old unpublished working papers and/or obscure journals.

4. Proofreading: Your next version should be as complete as possible. Indeed, this is the time to do a thorough edit of the paper by sending it to a professional editorial service.

5. Use of AI and AI-assisted technologies: If authors use generative AI or AI-assisted tools during the writing process, these tools should be limited to improving the language and readability of the manuscript. They must not be used to generate core content such as scientific insights, conclusions, or policy recommendations. Authors must disclose any use of such tools on the title page of their manuscript. Generative AI tools should not be credited as authors or co-authors, nor cited as such.
