| Item | Requirement | Revised package |
|---|---|---|
| Blinded article | no author names or affiliations in the paper | anonymized manuscript |
| Title page | separate, all authors and affiliations | 01_Title_Page.docx |
| Abstract | 150 words or less (editor: about 150 or less, forward-looking last line) | counted by builder |
| Length | research articles generally 5,000 to 7,000 words | body counted by builder; secondary results moved to the supplement |
| Exhibits | tables and figures within the text at the appropriate points | builder places each exhibit after its first citing paragraph |
| Format | MS Word or PDF for initial submission; journal template after acceptance | .docx |
| Replication | DOI link to a public replication folder before final acceptance | data availability statement says so |
| Revision | detailed list of responses, editor and referee separately; changes in blue | response letter; marked manuscript |
| AI use | limited to language and readability; disclosed on the title page | title page statement (author decision flagged) |
| Acknowledgements | title-page footnote acknowledging reviewer and editor comments | title page footnote |
